# Lakeland Cup — Design Context

## Users

**Twelve GMs** of the Lakeland Cup dynasty fantasy hockey league (founded 2013, currently in the 2025-26 season) and the occasional casual visitor a GM has shared a link with.

The site is **read-mostly**: GMs come back to verify protection status on a prospect they're about to trade for, look up who owns which future pick, scroll the championship list, or read the rules manual. The commissioner uses a password-gated admin area to record seasons, draft results, lottery outcomes, and trades.

Primary jobs:
- Check whether a player is still protected — and by whom.
- Confirm who holds a future pick (especially after multi-step trades).
- Browse championship history / franchise history / draft history.
- Read the league manual.
- (Commissioner) record new outcomes after a season, draft, or trade.

## Brand Personality

**Hallowed, lived-in, gold-leaf.** The site should feel like a small private trophy room with decades of patina — reverent but not stuffy, weighty but not corporate. The tone is clubhouse-confident: it knows its 12 readers personally. Hockey-native vocabulary throughout ("Trophy Case", "Original 5", "Defending Champion", "Runner-up", "GM"). Restraint is the brand: one logo, one date, one champion on the home page — not a KPI grid.

Voice in three words: **earned, dry, hockey-fluent**.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Dark, navy + gold + ice.** Dark theme is intentional — this is consumed in the evening after a draft, in the era of a small private league, not on a phone in bright sunlight. The dominant surface is `lake-blue-darkest` (≈ #0f1419), the dominant accent is `lake-gold` (warm 2013-trophy gold, not Tailwind yellow). Ice tones for primary text. State colors are derived from the brand hue family — `lake-success` sage, `lake-warning` amber, `lake-error` brand-red, `lake-silver`, `lake-bronze`, `lake-goalie` muted lavender.

Color authoring is OKLCH with `<alpha-value>` so opacity modifiers work. Body text uses the AAA-compliant `lake-ice-muted` token.

**Type**: System font stack today (the original Inter import was the dominant AI-tell and was removed). A distinctive display face paired with a refined body face would be the natural next typography upgrade — when chosen, it should NOT be Inter / DM Sans / Plus Jakarta / Instrument anything. Reach for something with weight and history — engraved-plaque energy, not designy.

**Layout**: Editorial page headers (gold uppercase eyebrow + bold h1 + short gold rule), feature-variant panels only on the most-important block per page (Trophy Case, defending champion's row, Active Franchises), default cards for everything else. Container queries on the Rafters jersey wall. Fluid type via `clamp()` on content pages.

**Anti-references**:
- ESPN / The Athletic / hero-overlay-photo sports sites.
- Yahoo Fantasy / ESPN Fantasy's dense form-and-table UI (the platforms the GMs use day-to-day — this site is the *opposite*).
- Crypto / SaaS dark-mode-with-cyan-glow dashboards.
- Generic Tailwind starter templates — hero + 3-column feature grid + testimonials.

**Distinctive elements that carry identity** (preserve these):
- **Original 5 pentagon badge** on `/history` — gold ring + ghosted "5" + five logos in pentagon.
- **Rafters jersey wall** — arena ceiling with spotlights, retired-jersey banners hanging.
- **Custom hockey iconography**: `LakeCupIcon` (tiered Stanley-Cup silhouette), `FaceOffIcon`, `PuckIcon`, `CrossedSticksIcon`, `WhistleIcon`.
- **Trades ledger** — date-as-spine layout with each team's "Received" column, crossed sticks between, conditional clauses surfaced with a gold lead-line.
- **Lottery reveal** with custom `lottery-anticipation` breathing animation (not `animate-pulse`).
- **Composite-only `champion-aura`** — pseudo-element radial halo on the defending champion's logo.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint signals authority.** Confident sites don't shout. One logo, one trophy, one date. Two cues for one fact, not four.
2. **The data is the story.** Editorial type and confident hierarchy let the championships, picks, and trades speak. Decoration earns its place.
3. **Hockey-native, not template-native.** Vocabulary, iconography, and structural metaphors come from the sport (face-off circle, crossed sticks, rafters, cup). Stock Heroicons and KPI grids are out.
4. **Hierarchy via weight, not by chrome.** Vary spacing, sizing, weight, and one feature-panel treatment per view. Don't differentiate by adding more visual noise.
5. **AAA accessibility is the floor.** Reduced motion respected globally and in `<style jsx>` blocks. Focus-visible ring on the brand gold. Skip-to-main. Form labels. All decorative SVGs `aria-hidden`. Body text hits AAA contrast.
